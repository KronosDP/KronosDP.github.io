Flagship AI Research Spotlight

Sequence Model Capacity: RWKV-v7 vs. Transformers — NVIDIA

NVIDIA AI Research • Remote • Jan 2025 – Jun 2025

Evaluated non-Transformer recurrent sequence model architectures (RWKV-v4 & RWKV-v7) against standard Transformers, LSTMs, and GRUs for formal regular language expressivity. Designed synthetic language datasets ($L_1$ Alternating Patterns, $L_2$ Parity Counting, $L_3$ Substring Matching, $L_4$ Language Unions) paired with 1–3 edit-distance near-miss hard negative perturbations to stress-test model decision boundaries. Implemented a custom PyTorch CUDA C++ WKV Operator (wkv_kernel.cu) for accelerated state transitions.
